The Sponsorship Registration Portal for the 6th RISC-V Summit China Will Officially Open at 9:00 PM on August 19 (CST / UTC+8) for Overseas Companies / International Sponsorship.

From October 18 to 20, 2026, RISC-V Summit China 2026 (6th RISC-V Summit China) will be held at the Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center (Futian). As an important annual exchange platform for the global open-source hardware and RISC-V ecosystem, this summit will bring together research institutions, supply chain enterprises, developer communities, system vendors, and ecosystem partners to jointly promote open computing ecosystem innovation and industrial application.

## Registration Details

- **Opening Time**
    - **Domestic / General Companies:** August 19, 2026 (Wednesday) at 9:00 AM (CST)
    - **Overseas Companies / International Sponsorship:** August 19, 2026 at 9:00 PM (CST / UTC+8)
- **Registration Rules:** Registration is served on a first-come, first-served basis. Quotas for sponsorship tiers, custom/special naming opportunities, and featured packages are limited and available until sold out. Booth locations and presentation time slots will be determined based on the order of contract signing. The organizing committee will contact interested organizations in sequence according to their registration submission time, partnership category, and benefit matching to confirm details.
- **Registration Link:** [https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/TSST6JZ](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/TSST6JZ)
